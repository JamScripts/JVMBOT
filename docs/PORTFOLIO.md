## Interview walkthrough

Explain the physical path from serial command to R3 to driver to wheel. Show an exact revision and repeatable demo. Describe one real failure, the evidence collected, what remains uncertain and how you verified recovery. Identify the next limitation without claiming autonomy or professional deployment experience.

Do not claim commercial fleet operations, implemented ROS navigation, autonomous security enforcement, measured speed/distance or production uptime without corresponding work and evidence.
