# JVMBOT protective body — design brief

The next enclosure is a measured engineering task. No production-ready CAD/STL or proven fit is included yet. Earlier print attempts are not sufficient to establish a fit for this assembled kit.

## Required measurements

- Chassis outline and usable mounting holes, measured from a consistent origin.
- Board footprints, mounting-hole spacing, component and connector heights.
- Battery holder, wire paths, wheel clearance and caster sweep.
- Pi USB, Ethernet, power and microSD access.
- Ultrasonic field of view and servo sweep if installed.

## Design constraints

Use a removable cover and nonconductive standoffs, protect wiring from wheels, allow ventilation and heat clearance, preserve access to reset/USB and the motor-power disconnect, and include JVMBOT branding. Model the actual current assembly, not an illustration.

Print a small mounting-hole/port-clearance coupon before a full body. Record material, layer height, supports, slicer time estimate and actual print duration. A print-time estimate requires the final sliced geometry; no estimate is claimed here.

Acceptance: no electrical contact or cable pinch, no wheel/servo interference, reachable power disconnect, serviceable boards and verified port alignment. Attach dimensioned CAD, STL/3MF, photos and fit notes.
